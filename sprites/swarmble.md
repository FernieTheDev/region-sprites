# Swarmble — Poke-issue-dex №93

*The Strategy Pokémon*

![Swarmble](swarmble.png)

**Type:** plan

**Rank:** hard

**Species:** Strategy Pokémon  
**Height:** 1.3 m   **Weight:** 17.8 kg

> Swarmble is the Overcrowd Pokémon: it swells whenever too many issuemon pile beneath a single town house on the Region map, stacking sprites into an unreadable heap. The map-view sibling of the board-level declutter effort (#84), it hunts for the right way to tame the crowd — overflow badges, drill-in towns, adaptive packing with culling, or an aggregate view past a capacity threshold — then designs and builds the fix so a crowded lane stays legible. It never lets an active or needs-attention creature vanish into the pile.

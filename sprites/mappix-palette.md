*The Strategy Pokémon*

![Mappix](issue-68-mappix.png)

**Type:** plan

**Rank:** medium

**Species:** Strategy Pokémon  
**Height:** 1.4 m   **Weight:** 24.5 kg

> A Palette-branch offspring of Mappix, this issuemon inherits the cartographer's lantern but trades map-dreaming for map-styling: it obsesses over how each gym town, route, and biome should *look* — the tile system, per-gym building stamps, and element/complexity-keyed habitats that make the overworld legible at a glance. A lineage variation of Mappix #60, it explores the aesthetic half of the Region-map vision so the League has a look to judge.

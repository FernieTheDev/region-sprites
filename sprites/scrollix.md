# Scrollix — Poke-issue-dex №65

*The Herald Pokémon*

![Scrollix](issue-65-scrollix.png)

**Type:** comms

**Rank:** medium

**Species:** Herald Pokémon  
**Height:** 1.7 m   **Weight:** 13.8 kg

> Scrollix is a scroll-tailed poke-issue that nests inside the Region Safari's click popup. Right now that card clips an issuemon's title to a tiny stub and swallows its description whole, so trainers can't tell what a creature actually is. Scrollix unfurls the hidden text — surfacing the full issue title (or a far longer character cap) plus two or three sentences of the description — and finds a tidy home for the status line (like "waiting in The Wild Area for Professor Triagon to triage & hatch it") so those helpful messages never get lost among the issue text.

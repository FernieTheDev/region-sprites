# Duolore — Poke-issue-dex №62

*The Scout Pokémon*

![Duolore](duolore.png)

**Type:** research

**Rank:** hard

**Species:** Scout Pokémon  
**Height:** 0.6 m   **Weight:** 11.3 kg

> Duolore is the Region's twin-minded lorekeeper — a research-type issuemon that cradles two rival knowledge systems, one in each hand, and weighs them against each other. It appears whenever the Region runs more than one upkeep loop at once, auditing each from every lane's point of view — what's good, what's better, what's missing — and never writes a line of code, only judgement. In the end it fuses its many findings into a single side-by-side verdict.

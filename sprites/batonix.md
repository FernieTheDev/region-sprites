# Batonix — Poke-issue-dex №73

*The Herald Pokémon*

![Batonix](batonix.png)

**Type:** comms

**Rank:** hard

**Species:** Herald Pokémon  
**Height:** 1.4 m   **Weight:** 7.0 kg

> Batonix is the Handoff Pokémon: a relay-runner whose tail is a glowing baton that must never be passed empty. In the Region today, when one issuemon is handed from lane to lane the baton arrives blank — the receiving agent learns nothing of *why* the card was routed or what it is expected to do next, so pairs like Forge Gym and Fusion Gym ping-pong the same creature back and forth in confusion. Batonix fixes this by making every handoff carry its reason: a structured Route-log line plus a short handoff note stating what was done, why it is being routed here, and the specific ask for the receiver — and it insists the receiver read and restate that note before acting, so shared state actually gets read and the ping-pong loops finally stop.

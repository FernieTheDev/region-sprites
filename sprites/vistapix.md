*The Tidy Pokémon*

![Vistapix](vistapix.png)

**Type:** cleanup

**Rank:** hard

**Species:** Tidy Pokémon  
**Height:** 0.9 m   **Weight:** 17.9 kg

> Vistapix is the Refinement Pokémon — a fastidious groomer of the Region's overworld map. It roams the Safari at dusk, hushing loud status banners into quiet chrome, trimming rambling issue bodies down to a legible sentence or two, and nudging any creature that has wandered off the edge of the screen safely back into view. It grows every map sprite to match its lanes-view cousins, strips the stray green ring from crowded clusters, and — its signature trick — drapes the whole world in a deep-navy night palette so every issuemon glows against the dark. Where Vistapix passes, the map simply reads better.

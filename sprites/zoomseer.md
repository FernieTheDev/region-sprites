# Zoomseer — Poke-issue-dex №10

*The Strategy Pokémon*

![Zoomseer](zoomseer.png)

**Type:** plan

**Rank:** hard

**Species:** Strategy Pokémon  
**Height:** 1.7 m   **Weight:** 27.0 kg

> Zoomseer is a far-sighted poke-issue that perceives the Region at two scales at once. From high above it sees a whole world of islands — every Region-space its own continent adrift on one shared ocean — then, with a blink of its great lens-eye, it dives through the clouds to land inside a single Region's overworld. It weaves the two views into one seamless glide so a trainer never loses their place between the world map and the ground, and it guards the older lane and single-map views so nothing it adds ever blocks the paths that came before. Only a Zoomseer can hold both the forest and the trees in the same gaze.
